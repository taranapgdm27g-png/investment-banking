04Experience

Revenue Assurance Intern, Paytm

One97 Communications, Noida. April to June 2026. Reporting to the General Manager, Revenue Assurance and Collections.

Internship report PDF
  • Receivables and working capital: B2B receivables across advertising, FMCG, D2C, government and other portfolios: ageing, DSO by vertical and expected credit loss provisioning for the monthly leadership MIS.
  • Cash application: daily payment application in SAP by UTR, resolving short payments, TDS and unapplied or excess receipts; the debtors book reconciled to the general ledger at Matched status in March 2026.
  • Reporting automation: a VBA engine that writes the monthly management commentary for every portfolio, cutting 4 hours to under a minute (99.6%).
  • Collections control: an ETA vs collections framework with performance bands and one-click emails to HODs and portfolio leads, replacing a 4-day manual cycle and saving 30 hours a month; replicated by the PPSL Revenue Assurance team.
  • Recovery and follow-up: recovery notices automated with an approval trail (3 hours to 30 minutes each, 40 a quarter), and a role-based ticketing system with ETA-breach alerts, built with another intern for the GM's team.
  • Result: about 80 hours a month freed across five frameworks, presented to all Revenue Assurance teams at Paytm's Automation Friday.

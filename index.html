About me

A finance mind with a writer's ear

I'm Tarana, a PGDM Finance and Analytics candidate at Great Lakes Gurgaon (Class of 2027). I came to finance from English Honours at Delhi University, so I care as much about explaining a number as calculating it.

I learn by building: the eight desks below, from bond pricing and an EBP book to a PF trust checked against the investment pattern. At Paytm I tracked what corporate client accounts committed against the cash that actually arrived, which is how a debt investor reads an issuer. 04Experience

Revenue Assurance Intern, Paytm

One97 Communications, Noida. April to June 2026. Reporting to the General Manager, Revenue Assurance and Collections.

Internship report PDF
  • Credit monitoring: B2B receivables across advertising, FMCG, D2C, government and other client accounts, reviewed monthly by ageing bucket, DSO and expected credit loss provisioning, with balances over 90 days flagged in the leadership MIS.
  • Committed vs realised cash: an ETA vs collections framework that tracks what each account committed against what arrived, sorts shortfalls into performance bands and sends one-click escalations to HODs and portfolio leads. It replaced a 4-day manual cycle with daily exception monitoring, saves 30 hours a month and was replicated by the PPSL Revenue Assurance team.
  • Reconciliation: daily payment application in SAP by UTR, resolving short payments, TDS and unapplied or excess receipts; the debtors book reconciled to the general ledger at Matched status in March 2026.
  • Leadership reporting: 23 separately maintained receivables trackers, a control and data-quality gap, rebuilt as one VBA-automated reporting layer that also writes the monthly management commentary for every portfolio, cutting 4 hours to under a minute (99.6%).
  • Recovery and follow-up: the recovery notice workflow redesigned end to end, with data compilation, annexures and approval routing automated (3 hours to 30 minutes each, 83% faster, 40 a quarter), and a role-based ticketing system with defined ownership, ETA-breach alerts and escalation, built with another intern for the GM's team.
  • Result: about 80 hours a month freed across five frameworks, presented to all Revenue Assurance teams at Paytm's Automation Friday.

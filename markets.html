MARKETS AND DERIVATIVES

Europe settles in one day from 11 October 2027

The EU, the UK and Switzerland move from T+2 to T+1 together. For teams in India supporting European settlements, the window to match, fund and fix a trade shrinks from a day and a half to a single evening.

  • Match on trade date: allocations, confirmations and settlement instructions must be complete the same evening, not the next day.
  • Fund and borrow earlier: cash for cross-currency trades and securities for short deliveries have to be in place overnight.
  • Fails cost more: with no second day to recover, unmatched trades fail and accrue CSDR cash penalties.
  • Automation decides it: straight-through processing and clean standing settlement instructions become the difference between settling and failing.

Certain securities financing transactions are exempt in the EU. The US moved to T+1 in May 2024.

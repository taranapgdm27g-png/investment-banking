The person behind the work

I'm curious, perceptive and naturally cheerful, and I genuinely enjoy talking to people. I ask a lot of questions, not just to get the answer, but because I like understanding how different people see the same situation.

That habit shapes how I work. The best insight in a process review rarely sits in the data alone; it sits in the conversation with the person who runs the process every day.

Orvelle

A fashion brand I built from scratch: a working store that shoppers use on their phones, and a Studio behind it that turns every tap into live customer, supply chain, marketing and finance numbers. I built it to see how one customer's journey, from an Instagram ad to a checkout, flows all the way into a P&L and a valuation.

Open the store Open the Studio Watch the customer journey
Scan to shop on your phone Or tap the code. Then open the Studio on a laptop and watch your visit appear live.

The store

  • Home page that rearranges itself around the styles you pick
  • 48 styles with filters, a style quiz, saved items and a bag
  • Checkout with UPI, cards, net banking and cash on delivery (payment simulated)
  • Phone login and pincode delivery check

The Studio, live

  • Every visit, login, product view and order appears within seconds, across phones and laptops
  • Journey funnel and who is in the store right now
  • Customers segmented by recency, frequency and spend, with the next best offer for each
  • Price and stock changes published straight to the store

The finance

  • Cohort-driven operating model and three statements
  • DCF against market multiples, risk and sensitivity, cap table and LBO
  • Unit economics, CAC and lifetime value, marginal ROAS
  • Landed cost, reorder planner and GMROI; everything exports to Excel

    Cash application and reconciliation

    Automated matching of bank credits to open invoices by reference and amount, with TDS recognition, short and excess payment handling, suspense for unmatched receipts, and a tie out to the GL. Based on the payment application process at Paytm.

    Matching rules

    TDS is deducted on the taxable value, so a 2% deduction on an invoice of ₹1,18,000 including GST is ₹2,000, not ₹2,360. The engine checks it that way.

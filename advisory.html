Portfolio Management · group project

Advising five investors, from risk profile to recommendation

Profiled five investors, built the efficient frontier for a 14-asset Indian universe and recommended, for each client, the portfolio that maximises their utility, then checked it against their goal.

  1. Profile. A seven-question risk questionnaire set each client's risk-aversion coefficient (A = 1, 3 or 4). Goals ran from ₹50 lakh to ₹12 crore, SIPs from ₹5,000 to ₹25,000 a month, and goal-implied required returns from 10.0% to 21.7% a year.
  2. Universe. 14 asset classes: government and corporate bonds, large, mid and small-cap equity, mutual funds, a multi-asset fund, gold, a REIT, a long-short AIF, ULIPs, child, pension and hybrid debt plans, each with expected return and risk; a correlation and covariance matrix for the 10 investable ones.
  3. Utility. U = E(R) − ½·A·σ² for A from 1 to 6, and each client's own return and risk beliefs compared with the market's.
  4. Optimise. Minimum-variance and maximum-Sharpe (tangency) portfolios with weights capped at 40%, the capital allocation line from a 7.5% risk-free rate, and 5,000 random-weight scenarios.
  5. Recommend and monitor. The highest-utility portfolio for each client's A, a goal feasibility check and a monitoring plan.

Each dot is one of the 5,000 scenarios (darker = higher Sharpe). Dashed: capital allocation line from the 7.5% risk-free rate through the tangency portfolio.

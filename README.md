A one-page portfolio built around Darashaw & Co.'s Campus Placements 2027 roles. It covers debt capital markets, retirement benefits, technical research, PCG, institutional dealing and strategy.

## Before you share it

1. **Add your résumé.** Save your Darashaw GLIM résumé in this folder as `Tarana_Bansal_Resume.pdf`. The Résumé buttons stay hidden until the file is there, then appear on their own.
2. **Host it.** On GitHub, upload every file in this folder to a repository or folder named `darashaw` and turn on GitHub Pages. The link will be `https://taranapgdm27g-png.github.io/darashaw/`.
3. **If the address is different**, search `index.html` for `taranapgdm27g-png.github.io/darashaw/` and replace it in the two places it appears (the `og:url` and `og:image` lines).

## The eight desks

1. Bonds and credit: price, duration, PV01 and an issuer credit view
2. EBP book: bids, cut-off yield and allotment on the electronic book platform
3. PF trust: the investment pattern, the 8.25% hurdle and a rate shock
4. Technical lab: moving averages, Bollinger Bands, RSI and MACD, with a written call; paste real closing prices to test it
5. Valuation: Dabur India DCF and comparables
6. Portfolio: efficient frontier and rebalancing bot
7. Wealth: goal planner with 1,000 market paths
8. Project finance: DSCR and debt sizing for a 100 MW solar plant

## Keeping it current

- **Market notes:** search `index.html` for `const NOTES=` and edit or add a note in the same format. Market levels on the page are as of 15 October 2026.
- **Credentials:** search for `const CREDS=`. Paste a verification link into `url:""` to show a "Verify" link.

Desk inputs are illustrative except the Dabur and L&T figures, which come from your own models.

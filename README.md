# Tarana Bansal: investment banking portfolio

Live site: https://taranapgdm27g-png.github.io/investment-banking/

PGDM Finance and Analytics, Great Lakes Institute of Management, Gurgaon (2027).

What is here:

- **index.html**: the main portfolio. Selected work (Dabur India initiation, Larsen & Toubro valuation, Orvelle, portfolio optimisation, Paytm, controls testing), the Deal Studio (nine live desks: valuation, M&A, LBO, IPO, bonds and credit, project finance, restructuring, wealth, portfolio), experience, certifications and job simulations, and notes on Indian deals and markets.
- **orvelle.html**: Orvelle, a D2C fashion brand with a live store and a finance Studio.
- **markets.html**: markets operations workbench (seven models of a bank's markets back office).
- **assurance.html**: controls monitoring on 12,095 synthetic invoices.
- **advisory.html**: client advisory desk (risk profile, goal planner, client book).
- The Excel models, decks, reports and résumé that the pages link to.

Everything runs in the browser; no build step. Data in the live tools is illustrative or synthetic unless stated otherwise.
